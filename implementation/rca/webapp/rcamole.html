    Confidence score

    How cleanly a single segment explains the anomaly — high explanatory power plus a clear lift over the segment's size. Low confidence means the drill-down found nothing that stood out, and the finding is reported as broad-based rather than forced.

    LMDI decomposition

    Revenue is decomposed into Requests × Fill Rate × Render Rate × eCPM using a log-mean divisia index, so every factor's contribution to the change is exact and the four contributions always sum to the total delta.

    Explanatory power & lift

    Explanatory power is the share of the movement one segment accounts for. Lift compares that to the segment's size — a lift near 1× means it simply moved with everyone else; well above 1× means it moved disproportionately, and is the bar a segment must clear to be named a root cause.

    Severity levels

    HIGH: material revenue or fill-rate impact with a confidently localized cause. MEDIUM: a real, measurable deviation, often on a secondary metric. LOW: a contained move affecting a small share of traffic.
